      THE 30-SECOND BRIEFING

      Fast is good.
      Smart is faster.

      DRIVE Hold WASD / arrow keys to steer and accelerate. Release to coast. Shift or the large Brake button slows you down. Touch players use the direction pad.

      RACE Choose 2, 5 or 7 laps on your selected circuit. Follow road arrows and all checkpoints, then cross the finish. Your car has a white ring; the minimap shows the full route.

      SABOTAGE Space fires the selected skill; Q / E switches skills. 1 / 2 / 3 or tapping a skill fires it directly. SHOOT assists your aim at a nearby rival ahead; an orange target marker shows a lock. Shots still stop at walls. BOMB drops behind you and flashes for 1.4 seconds before exploding. NITRO boosts your own speed. Shots slow you 20% for 0.7 seconds; bomb blasts slow you 45% for 1.8 seconds. Keep driving; no car stops or is destroyed.

      PLAY FAIR Start with three charges, hold five at most, and collect shared lightning pickups. Your weapons do not hit your own car. Hits have recovery protection; no contact between cars. Choose Race the Computer for solo practice against a clearly labeled CPU; multiplayer rooms contain real players only.
