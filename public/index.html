      THE 30-SECOND BRIEFING

      Fast is good.
      Smart is faster.

      DRIVE Hold WASD / arrow keys to steer and accelerate. Release to coast. Shift or the large Brake button slows you down. Touch players use the direction pad.

      RACE Two laps on a long 12-turn circuit. Follow road arrows and all 12 checkpoints, then cross the finish. Your car has a white ring; the minimap shows the full route.

      SABOTAGE Space fires the selected skill; Q / E switches skills. 1 / 2 / 3 or tapping a skill fires it directly. SHOOT fires a visible shot straight ahead. BOMB drops behind you and flashes for 1.4 seconds before exploding. NITRO boosts your own speed. Hits cause a brief slowdown; no car is destroyed.

      PLAY FAIR Start with three charges, hold five at most, and collect shared lightning pickups. Your weapons do not hit your own car. Hits have recovery protection; no contact between cars. Choose Race the Computer for solo practice against a clearly labeled CPU; multiplayer rooms contain real players only.
